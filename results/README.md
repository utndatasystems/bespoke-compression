# Measurement evidence

Final figure input records are under [`paper/figures/`](../paper/figures/README.md).
Historical raw trials, qualification notes and per-codec provenance remain under
`synthesized-code/**/data/` and `evidence/`. Cost CSV/JSON records retain their
original dates and accounting scope.

[`reproducibility/`](reproducibility/README.md) separates prior cleanup checks
from the dated final release verification. Saved results describe the supplied
implementations and original experiment conditions; they do not certify new
models or imply identical timing on a different host.
